01 / WHY IT MATTERS

The ocean keeps
our world alive.

From the air we breathe to the food systems we depend on, the ocean supports life in ways we often overlook. But pollution, habitat loss, and climate change are putting this delicate balance at risk.

Seavari is a space to learn about these challenges, understand marine ecosystems, and turn awareness into meaningful action.

01 — THE EVERYDAY THREAT

Plastic pollution

Plastic waste travels through rivers and coastlines into the sea. It can entangle marine animals, be mistaken for food, and break down into tiny particles that remain in the environment.

WHAT CAN HELP

Choose reusable items, avoid unnecessary packaging, dispose of waste responsibly, and support local clean-up efforts.
