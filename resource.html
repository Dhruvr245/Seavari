The Truth About Plastic Fragments

Plastic fragments form when larger plastic items break down under sunlight, waves, and wind. They can travel through rivers and oceans, settle on beaches or the seafloor, and be mistaken for food by marine wildlife. Over time, fragments can become microplastics and even smaller particles. Reducing disposable plastic use and preventing litter from entering waterways are important steps toward limiting this pollution.
